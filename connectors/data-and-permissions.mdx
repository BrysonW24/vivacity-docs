## Before sharing information with AI

Use fictional or sanitised examples while learning. Remove personal, customer, employee, financial and confidential information unless you have authority to share it and understand how the selected tool will handle it.

A business subscription, a consumer chat account and an API service can have different terms and controls. Check the actual product, account settings and provider documentation rather than assuming one policy applies to every version.

## For a scoped setup or workflow

Agree:

- Which information is needed, why and who may access it.
- Which systems and third-party providers will process it.
- The permissions and any actions the workflow may take.
- How long relevant records are kept and how access or deletion requests are handled.
- Who reviews the output and what happens when the task fails.

You own your accounts and enter credentials and payment details yourself. Do not send passwords, full payment details or API keys by email or paste them into a general chat.

## Read the permission request

Confirm the right account and the actual scopes requested. A tool that needs to read a document should not receive unrelated access without a clear reason and your agreement.
