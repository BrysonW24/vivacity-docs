Connect **Google Sheets** so Vivacity can see your workspace tools and help you act on it. Your data stays **read-only** while Vivacity learns. Anything it suggests is **drafted for you to approve** - it never sends or changes anything in Google Sheets without your sign-off.

## What Vivacity does with Google Sheets

- Read the documents and records you share.
- Summarise and answer questions over them.
- Draft updates for your approval.

## What syncs

Vivacity reads the following from Google Sheets (read-only):

- Rows from the sheets you share (e.g. a CRM or lead list)
- Used to import contacts and records, read-only

<Note>Vivacity only ever **reads** the data you connect. It does not write back to Google Sheets unless you explicitly approve an action.</Note>

## Connect Google Sheets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connectors">
    In Vivacity, go to **Settings → Connectors**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Google Sheets">
    Find **Google Sheets** under **Productivity** and select **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorise with Google Sheets">
    You'll be redirected to Google Sheets to sign in and approve **read-only** access. Vivacity never sees your Google Sheets password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to share">
    Pick the account, store or property to sync, then confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Vivacity runs a first sync. You'll see a **Connected** badge when it's ready (usually a few minutes).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Permissions & security

- **Read-only by default.** Setup grants read access only.
- **You stay in control.** Every suggested action is drafted for your approval before anything happens.
- **Encrypted & revocable.** Credentials are stored encrypted; disconnect any time in **Settings → Connectors**.

See [Data & permissions](/connectors/data-and-permissions) for the full picture.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The connection failed or won't authorise">
    Make sure you're signed in to the correct Google Sheets account with admin access, then retry. If a pop-up was blocked, allow pop-ups for Vivacity and try again. Still stuck? See [Connection issues](/troubleshooting/connection-issues).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My Google Sheets data looks out of date">
    Vivacity syncs on a schedule. Trigger a manual sync from **Settings → Connectors → Google Sheets → Sync now**, or see [Data & sync](/troubleshooting/data-and-sync).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see “Reconnect required”">
    Google Sheets access can expire or be revoked. Open the connector and select **Reconnect** to restore it - your history is kept.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
