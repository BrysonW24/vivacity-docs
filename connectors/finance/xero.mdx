## What connecting Xero lets Vivacity do

When you open a customer's enquiry, owners and admins see that customer's recent invoices from Xero beside it, and whether anything is overdue. That helps you reply knowing where the account stands.

## Exactly what it reads

You choose **one** Xero organisation when you connect. Then, only for an enquiry you pick, Vivacity reads:

- the Xero contact with the same email address as the enquiry;
- up to 10 of that contact's sales invoices from the last 12 months;
- whether that contact has anything overdue.

Xero's permission screen covers all contacts, invoices, quotes and credit notes. Our code reads only the items above.

## What it writes

Nothing. Vivacity has no way to change your Xero.

## What it never does

- It never writes to Xero.
- Invoice details never go into a reply and are never given to an AI model.
- Only owners and admins in your workspace see them. Other team members do not.
- It never shows one business's Xero to another business.

## Who can connect it

An owner or admin of your workspace, from **Connections**, after reading and agreeing to the words on the Connect screen. You sign in on Xero's own page; Vivacity never sees your Xero password.

## How to disconnect

In Vivacity, open **Connections**, then **Disconnect** on the Xero card. Straight away we:

- end our access in Xero;
- delete your sign-in from our sign-in service;
- delete this connection's working copies.

To double check, look at the connected apps in your Xero and make sure Vivacity is no longer listed.

## What is kept, and for how long

| What | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Working copies of what we read | 14 days, or until you disconnect |
| Our receipts (record ids and checksums only, no invoice details) | Until you close your workspace |
| Your consent record (which words you agreed to, and when) | Until you close your workspace |
| Our sign-in service's request logs, which include the email looked up | Up to 15 days |

## Who handles it

- **Vivacity** stores it with Supabase in Sydney.
- **Nango**, our sign-in service, runs outside Australia. It holds your Xero sign-in and logs the email we look up for 15 days.
- **Lovable**, our app host, may process it outside Australia.

## Something else we may ask later

We have also built a way for Vivacity to learn about your business from your Xero contact list. It is switched off, and it will never run without its own wording and your separate agreement.
