A connection lets one tool exchange information or actions with another. It can help with repeated work, but it also introduces permissions, cost and responsibility.

Start with the task. Sometimes a sample document or manual export is enough; sometimes a scoped integration makes sense.

## How to use this library

The tool pages below help you prepare a task, ask useful questions and plan access. They are **educational references**, rather than a catalogue of ready-made Vivacity integrations. Product names and logos identify the tools being discussed; they do not imply a partnership or endorsement.

For an actual connection, we check the provider's current options, your account, the permissions needed and a suitable test before agreeing the work.
