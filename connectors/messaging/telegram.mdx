Connect **Telegram** so Vivacity can see your messaging channels and help you act on it. Your data stays **read-only** while Vivacity learns. Anything it suggests is **drafted for you to approve** - it never sends or changes anything in Telegram without your sign-off.

## What Vivacity does with Telegram

- Draft messages and alerts in your voice.
- Route conversations and flag what needs you.
- Never send without your explicit approval.

## What syncs

Vivacity reads the following from Telegram (read-only):

- Message threads you connect, for drafting
- Delivery and read status where available

<Note>Vivacity only ever **reads** the data you connect. It does not write back to Telegram unless you explicitly approve an action.</Note>

## Connect Telegram

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connectors">
    In Vivacity, go to **Settings → Connectors**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Telegram">
    Find **Telegram** under **Messaging** and select **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your Telegram API key">
    Paste your Telegram API key when prompted. Vivacity stores it **encrypted** and uses it only to read the data you approve. (Find the key in your Telegram account settings.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to share">
    Pick the account, store or property to sync, then confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Vivacity runs a first sync. You'll see a **Connected** badge when it's ready (usually a few minutes).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Permissions & security

- **Read-only by default.** Setup grants read access only.
- **You stay in control.** Every suggested action is drafted for your approval before anything happens.
- **Encrypted & revocable.** Credentials are stored encrypted; disconnect any time in **Settings → Connectors**.

See [Data & permissions](/connectors/data-and-permissions) for the full picture.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The connection failed or won't authorise">
    Make sure you're signed in to the correct Telegram account with admin access, then retry. If a pop-up was blocked, allow pop-ups for Vivacity and try again. Still stuck? See [Connection issues](/troubleshooting/connection-issues).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My Telegram data looks out of date">
    Vivacity syncs on a schedule. Trigger a manual sync from **Settings → Connectors → Telegram → Sync now**, or see [Data & sync](/troubleshooting/data-and-sync).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see “Reconnect required”">
    Telegram access can expire or be revoked. Open the connector and select **Reconnect** to restore it - your history is kept.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
