---
title: "Manage access to your tools"
description: "Keep ownership, permissions and removal clear for each connection."
icon: "sliders"
---

Keep a simple connection record alongside the instructions for the specific workflow. The provider's account settings remain the place to inspect the access it has granted.

## Record what matters

- The tool and account involved, plus the business owner responsible.
- The application or workflow receiving access and the task it serves.
- Permissions granted, including anything it can write, send or spend.
- Where the secure credential is managed, without copying the secret into the record.
- The test, review point and instructions for stopping the workflow.

## When something changes

Review access when a person leaves, an account changes, a task stops or a provider asks for new permissions. Pause dependent workflows if you are unsure whether the access remains appropriate.

## Remove access deliberately

Use the provider's current controls to revoke the relevant app, key or token, and stop any associated scheduled workflow. Revocation and deletion of data already copied are separate tasks; confirm both where needed.

Changing credentials or reconnecting can affect other work. Check what depends on the connection before making changes.
