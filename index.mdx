## What Vivacity does

Vivacity is an AI co-pilot for business owners. Connect your store, ads, inbox, CRM and accounting, and Vivacity turns the noise into a clear picture and a short list of things worth doing.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="See" icon="eye">
    One place that pulls together revenue, leads, ads, cash and customers from the tools you already use.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Understand" icon="lightbulb">
    Plain-English explanations of what changed and why - no dashboards to decode.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Act" icon="circle-check">
    The next best actions, drafted in your voice, ready for you to approve.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
