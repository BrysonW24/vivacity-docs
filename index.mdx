## What Vivacity does

Vivacity is a workspace for small and medium businesses. It helps you deal with customer enquiries: you paste in an enquiry, Vivacity drafts a reply and pulls out the details, and you approve the exact words before anything is used. You send the reply yourself.

It never sends a message for you, and it never changes another app without an owner's or admin's approval of the exact change.
