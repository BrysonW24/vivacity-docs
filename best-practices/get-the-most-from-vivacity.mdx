## Choose one repeatable task

Pick work you understand well enough to check. A small draft, summary or checklist makes a better first exercise than handing an AI assistant your entire business.

## Keep a simple method

- Save the brief and a fictional example.
- Record what a good result looks like.
- Check facts, missing details and tone each time.
- Update the prompt when a mistake shows you what was unclear.

## Review the value

Notice what became easier and what still needed work. If you measure time or quality, compare the same task and include the time spent checking and correcting the result. Your observations help decide whether to keep using the method.

## Add complexity when it earns its place

More subscriptions, connected accounts or automation are useful only if they help the task. If the manual approach is working, keep it simple.
