An AI draft can help you prepare. You remain responsible for deciding whether it is accurate and appropriate to use.

## A practical review

1. **Check the facts.** Compare names, dates, prices, figures and commitments with the source.
2. **Check the fit.** Does the result suit your audience, tone and intended outcome?
3. **Check the gaps.** Ask what is assumed, missing or uncertain.
4. **Decide the next step.** Edit, reject, investigate or use the result yourself.

## For a connected workflow

Before a workflow changes a system, publishes content, spends money or contacts someone, agree what it is allowed to do and who approves it. Begin with drafts or test information where practical.

The review process, records, failure handling and any permitted automatic steps are defined for that specific engagement. Do not assume a general-purpose AI assistant will enforce your business rules unless the actual setup has been checked.
