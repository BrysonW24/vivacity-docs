## Alerts

Vivacity watches your connected data for things worth your attention - a spike in refunds, a drop in ad performance, a big overdue invoice, an unusual surge in leads - and tells you in plain English, with the likely cause and a suggested next step. You choose what's worth an alert; thresholds are tuned to your business, not generic defaults.

## Reports

Turn the current picture into a clean summary you can share - a weekly performance recap, a cash-flow snapshot, a lead-source breakdown. Reports read from the same consistent numbers as your briefings, so what you share matches what you see.
