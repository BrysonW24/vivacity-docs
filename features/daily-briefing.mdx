A short check-in can help you organise your work. You can start manually, using a sanitised task list or a fictional example in your chosen AI assistant.

## A repeatable exercise

> Using only this sample task list, summarise what is due, what is blocked and what needs a decision. Put missing information in a separate list. Do not invent dates, owners or completed work.

Compare the summary with the original list. Fix any mistakes before using it to plan your day.

## Make the routine useful

- Keep the source and date beside the summary.
- Distinguish facts, assumptions and questions.
- Choose one next step and a person responsible.
- Review whether the check-in is helping before adding more information or tools.

## If you want it automated

An automated briefing needs its own agreed sources, permissions, frequency, cost and tests. A general guide is not a promise that a scheduled briefing or monitoring service is included in setup and coaching.
