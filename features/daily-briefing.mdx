The Daily Briefing is the fastest way to know where your business stands - without opening five dashboards. It's the first thing most owners check.

## What's in it

- **What changed** - revenue, orders, leads, ad spend, cash - versus what's normal for you.
- **Why it changed** - the likely drivers, explained in plain English.
- **What needs you** - the handful of things worth acting on today, each with a suggested next step.

## How to use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your briefing">
    Find it on your home screen, or in the notification you get when it's ready each morning.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the highlights">
    Start at the top - the briefing is ordered by what matters most for your business right now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act or ask">
    Approve a suggested action, or tap through to [Ask Vivacity](/features/ask-vivacity) to dig into any line.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Connecting more sources makes the briefing sharper. Revenue plus ads plus CRM lets Vivacity connect cause and effect - e.g. "leads dipped because Friday's campaign spend dropped." See [connectors](/connectors/introduction).
</Tip>

## Make it yours

- Adjust what the briefing leads with as your priorities change.
- Set the delivery time that suits you.
- Quiet topics you don't care about so the signal stays high.
