A clear brief helps whether you use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another suitable tool. You can also ask Vivacity for help choosing a method that fits your work.

## Use a brief with four parts

- **Context:** who the work is for and what the tool needs to know.
- **Task:** the result you want it to prepare.
- **Boundaries:** facts to preserve, information to leave out and things it must ask you.
- **Format:** a short draft, table, checklist or set of questions.

## Try this

> I run a fictional local gardening business. Draft a friendly reply to an enquiry about a regular visit. Ask for the suburb, garden size and preferred day. Do not invent a price or confirm a booking. Keep it under 100 words.

Read the reply as if you were sending it. Does it ask the right questions? Has it invented anything? Is it clear what happens next?

## Continue the conversation

Ask it to shorten the draft, explain an assumption or show what is missing. Check factual claims against the original source. A confident answer can still be wrong.
