Gemini is Google's AI assistant. Google's [Gemini Apps guide](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13275745?hl=en) explains starting a conversation and account requirements.

## A useful first exercise

Open the [official Gemini site](https://gemini.google.com) and try:

> Help a fictional local florist prepare for a community event. Create a checklist for confirming the stall, choosing stock and preparing a short announcement. Do not invent the event date, fee, quantities or organiser details. Put missing information in a separate list.

Compare the result with what you actually asked. Ask it to explain any assumption before you use the checklist.

## Before linking other tools

Check which product and account you are using, its available features, permissions and current costs. Do not assume another Google account or service is connected simply because you use Gemini.
