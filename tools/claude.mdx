Claude is Anthropic's AI assistant. Its [official getting-started guide](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8114491-get-started-with-claude) explains access and prompting.

## A useful first exercise

Open the [official Claude site](https://claude.ai) and try:

> Turn these fictional meeting notes into a checklist. Separate decisions, open questions and suggested next steps. Do not invent dates or owners. Notes: We want to try a monthly customer newsletter. We have not chosen a date, owner or email tool. We need to decide what readers would find useful.

Ask it to make the checklist shorter, then compare it with the notes. Has it treated an undecided idea as a commitment?

## Before using real material

Check the product, account settings and provider terms for the work you intend to do. Features, limits and charges can vary. Share only information you are authorised to provide and check the result before using it.
