ChatGPT is OpenAI's conversational AI assistant. You can describe a task, review its response and ask follow-up questions. OpenAI's [getting-started guide](https://openai.com/academy/getting-started/) covers the basics.

## A useful first exercise

Open the [official ChatGPT site](https://chatgpt.com) and use a fictional example:

> Draft a friendly reply for a fictional local gardener. Ask an enquirer for their suburb, garden size and preferred day. Do not invent a price or confirm availability. Keep it under 100 words.

Then ask: **What information is still missing before I could send this?**

Check that it preserved the facts and did not invent commitments. Edit the tone to suit you.
