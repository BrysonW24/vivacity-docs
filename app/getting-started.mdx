<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Enter your email and the six-digit code we send you, then name your business and choose what you call your work: a job, a ticket, a booking or an order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the list on Home">
    Home shows a short **Getting set up** list. Each line ticks itself once it is done, and each one opens the screen that does it. The list goes away when everything is ticked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The list on Home

| Step | Where you do it |
| --- | --- |
| Say what your business does | **Settings**, then **Business profile** |
| Connect an app you use | **Connections** |
| Add the times and prices you offer | **Settings**, then **Book and quote** |
| Vivacity checks your business | Nothing to do. We check each new business before replies can be emailed from the app. |
| Get your first enquiry | **Inbox**, where your enquiry form's address is |

<Tip>
Say what your business does in one or two sentences, the way you would to a new customer. Every draft reply uses it.
</Tip>
