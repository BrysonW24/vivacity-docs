---
title: "Emailing replies"
description: "Why a new business is checked before it can email from the app."
icon: "paper-plane"
---

Before replies can be emailed from the app, Vivacity checks each new business. This stops anyone using the app to send email in another business's name.

Until your business is checked, **Approve** gives you the reply to copy and send from your own email. Once it is checked, **Approve and send** emails it to the customer who enquired through your form, from Vivacity with your business's name on it. Their reply comes back to you.

Home's **Getting set up** list ticks **Vivacity checks your business** when it is done. There is nothing you need to do.
