Every enquiry lands in **Inbox**, marked with where it came from.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Your website form" icon="globe">
    Your business has its own enquiry form. Its address is on the **Your enquiry form** card in **Inbox**: put it on your website or send it to a customer. You can switch the form off on the same card.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Email" icon="envelope">
    Emails sent to your business's enquiry address are filed in **Inbox** like any other enquiry.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Text and phone" icon="phone">
    Pilot businesses can have a number set up by Vivacity. A customer who texts it gets a reply from your automated assistant, at any hour, using only what you have told it. A caller hears that the call is recorded and can leave a message. The message is written out as text and filed in **Inbox**, and the recording is then deleted. Texts say they come from an automated assistant, and anyone can reply STOP.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Replying

Open an enquiry to see the reply the assistant drafted. Edit it if you like, then approve it. Nothing is sent to a customer by email until you approve it.

What arrives out of hours is waiting for you in the morning, with a short summary on **Home**.
