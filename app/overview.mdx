<Note>
The app is in an invite-only pilot with a small number of Australian businesses. No price is listed here.
</Note>

Customers reach you by your website form, email, text or a phone call. Each one lands in one Inbox, an assistant drafts a reply, and nothing goes out until you approve it. Connect the apps you already use and the app can read from them, answer questions about them and, with your yes, update them.
