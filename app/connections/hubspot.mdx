## What it does today

When an enquiry comes in, the app can offer to add the customer to HubSpot. It checks HubSpot first, shows you what it will add or update, and does nothing until you press **Approve**. Afterwards it reads the contact back to confirm it was saved.

You can also ask the **Assistant** to add someone to HubSpot. It asks for your **Approve** in the same way.

## Connect

Open **Connections**, choose **HubSpot** and press **Connect**, then sign in on HubSpot's own screen and choose your account. To disconnect, press **Disconnect** on the same card.
