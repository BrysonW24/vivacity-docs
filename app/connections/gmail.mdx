## What it does today

You can connect Gmail. The app records which account is connected. It does not read your email yet.

## Coming

Learning how you write from the emails you have sent, so draft replies sound like you. Anything it learns is shown to you to approve first.

## Connect

Open **Connections**, choose **Gmail** and press **Connect**, then sign in on Google's own screen. To disconnect, press **Disconnect** on the same card.
