## What it does today

- Reads your contacts, invoices and payments. Nothing in Xero is changed.
- **Home** shows what you are owed and how much of it is overdue.
- Ask the **Assistant** "Who owes me money?" for the total and the overdue invoices by name. Each answer says it was read from Xero.
- On an overdue invoice, the app can write a reminder for you to check and copy. It does not send it.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connections">Choose **Xero** and press **Connect**.</Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to Xero">Sign in on Xero's own screen and allow access.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose your organisation">If you have more than one, pick the one for this business.</Step>
</Steps>

To disconnect, open **Connections**, choose **Xero** and press **Disconnect**.
