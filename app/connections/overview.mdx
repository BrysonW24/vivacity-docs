Open **Connections**, choose an app and press **Connect**. You sign in on the app's own screen; Vivacity never sees your password. You can disconnect from the same card at any time.

The app never changes anything in a connected app without your **Approve**.

## What works today

| App | What it does today |
| --- | --- |
| [Xero](/app/connections/xero) | Reads contacts, invoices and payments. Shows what you are owed and answers money questions. |
| [HubSpot](/app/connections/hubspot) | Adds or updates a contact after you approve it. |
| [Google Calendar](/app/connections/google-calendar) | Connects. Nothing is read from it yet. |
| [Gmail](/app/connections/gmail) | Connects. Nothing is read from it yet. |
