## What it does today

You can connect Google Calendar. The app records which account is connected. It does not read your events yet.

## Coming

Reading your events into **Calendar**, so your bookings and Google Calendar sit side by side.

## Connect

Open **Connections**, choose **Google Calendar** and press **Connect**, then sign in on Google's own screen. To disconnect, press **Disconnect** on the same card.
