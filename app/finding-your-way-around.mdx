The menu on the left has a few places. Inside each, tabs across the top take you deeper. On a phone the menu is along the bottom, with **More** for the rest.

| Place | What it is for | Tabs inside |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Home** | What needs you today, a search across your records, and quick actions | |
| **Inbox** | Every enquiry, from every channel, with its draft reply | All enquiries, Needs a reply, Drafts waiting, No reply needed, Approvals, Decided |
| **Customers** | Your customers and their history | Contacts, Customers, Feedback, Support |
| **Work** | What you have to do and when | Tasks, Scheduled work, Workflows |
| **Calendar** | Scheduled work, dated tasks and call backs together | |
| **Money** | Invoices and expenses, and services from Vivacity | Invoices, Expenses, Services |
| **Insights** | How the business is going | Reports, Your week, Marketing |
| **Connections** | The apps you connect | |
| **Settings** | Your team and how the assistant works | Team and account, Business profile, Your voice, Memory, Assistant, Book and quote |

## Doing things by asking

The **Assistant** button at the top right opens a chat on any screen. Ask it to add a task, draft a reply or tell you who owes you money. When it wants to change something, it shows you the change and waits for **Approve**.
