## Allow, Ask or Never

In **Settings**, then **Assistant**, every action the assistant can take has three choices:

- **Ask**: it shows you the change and waits for your **Approve**. Every action starts here.
- **Allow**: it runs straight away.
- **Never**: it is turned off, even if you ask in the chat.

Some actions, such as changing a record in a connected app, always ask and cannot be changed.

## Booking and quoting

In **Settings**, then **Book and quote**, list the times you take work and the prices you charge. The automated assistant offers only those times and states only those prices. With nothing listed, it books and quotes nothing and takes the customer's details instead.

## Your voice and memory

**Settings**, then **Business profile**, **Your voice** and **Memory** are where you teach the assistant how your business runs and how you like to sound. It uses what you have approved there, and nothing else, when it drafts.
