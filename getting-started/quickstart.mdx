<Note>
  Vivacity is by invitation while we test it. If you have not had an invitation, email [hello@vivacityai.com.au](mailto:hello@vivacityai.com.au).
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in" icon="right-to-bracket">
    Use the sign-in details from your invitation. You can sign in with your password or with a link we email you. There is no way to create an account yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Enquiries" icon="inbox">
    Paste in a customer enquiry, for example an email or a message from your website form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the draft" icon="pen">
    Vivacity drafts a reply and pulls out the details, such as the customer's name and what they asked for. Change anything you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the exact words" icon="circle-check">
    Nothing is used until you approve it. Then copy the reply and send it yourself, the way you normally would.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- While we test, use made-up enquiries unless we have agreed with you that real ones can go in.
- Vivacity never sends a message for you.
- Connections to ServiceM8 and Xero are not available yet. See [ServiceM8](/connectors/servicem8) and [Xero](/connectors/finance/xero).
