Connecting your first tool is the moment Vivacity comes to life. Here's how, and what happens next.

## Which tool should I connect first?

Pick the one that best reflects how your business makes money:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Run an online store?" icon="cart-shopping">
    Start with **Shopify**, **WooCommerce** or **BigCommerce** - orders, products and customers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Service or B2B?" icon="address-book">
    Start with your **CRM** (HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce) or **accounting** (Xero, QuickBooks).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Spend on ads?" icon="bullhorn">
    Add **Meta Ads** or **Google Ads** to see spend against results.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Live in your inbox?" icon="envelope">
    Connect **Gmail** or **Outlook** so Vivacity can help with customer email.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Connect it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connectors">
    Go to **Settings → Connectors**. Connectors are grouped by category - Ecommerce, Advertising, CRM, Finance and more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your tool and select Connect">
    Find your tool and select **Connect**. Each tool has its own help page with exact steps - see the [connector library](/connectors/introduction).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorise read-only access">
    You'll either sign in to the tool (OAuth) or paste an API key. Vivacity requests **read-only** access and stores credentials encrypted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to share, then confirm">
    Pick the store, account or property to sync and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens during the first sync

- Vivacity reads your recent history to build a baseline (a few minutes for most accounts; large histories take longer).
- You'll see a **Connected** badge, then a notification when your first briefing is ready.
- Nothing is changed in your tool - this is a read.

<Note>
  Some connectors show a **Manual fallback** state. That means a direct connection isn't available yet, and you can import the data manually (for example via a Google Sheet) until it is.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting the first connection

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The authorise pop-up didn't open or got blocked">
    Allow pop-ups for Vivacity and try again. Make sure you're logged in to the right account with admin rights.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says connected but I see no data yet">
    The first sync may still be running. Give it a few minutes; see [Data & sync](/troubleshooting/data-and-sync).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
