description: "Give an AI task enough context without sharing more than it needs."

An AI task may need a short brief, a sample document or a small table. Begin with the smallest useful example. A direct connection to a business system is a separate decision.

## A simple starting point

1. Write down the question or task.
2. Identify the information needed to answer it.
3. Use fictional details or remove identifying and confidential information.
4. Check that you are allowed to share the remaining material with the chosen tool.
5. Ask for a draft and compare it with the original source.

For a customer reply, a fictional enquiry and your approved service facts may be enough. For a checklist, a sample process may be enough. Neither needs access to your entire inbox.

## If copying a sample is not enough

A scoped integration may be appropriate for repeated work. Before authorising it, agree the account, data, permissions, actions, cost and how access will be removed. Check what the provider supports for your account.
