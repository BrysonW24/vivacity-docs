## Setup and coaching

We start with your work, rather than a list of AI features. Together, we choose a suitable tool, get you set up and practise a task that matters to you. You keep the account and a guide you can return to.

## Going further

Some tasks need more than a useful prompt. A connection, workflow, report or custom tool may help, but it needs a clear purpose and a way to check whether it works.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Understand the task">Describe the problem, current process, people involved and a useful outcome.</Step>
  <Step title="Agree the smallest useful scope">Set out the work, access, responsibilities, testing, price and support before building.</Step>
  <Step title="Test and review">Check the result against the agreed examples and acceptance criteria. A demonstration is a chance to learn, rather than proof of a business outcome.</Step>
  <Step title="Decide the next step">Keep it, improve it, narrow it or stop. Ongoing support is optional and agreed separately.</Step>
</Steps>

## You stay in control

You decide which accounts and information are involved. We agree permissions and review points before work changes a system or contacts someone. Setup and coaching do not require you to connect every tool you use.
