## What to include when asking for help

- The tool and type of account you use.
- The task, expected result and what actually happened.
- The error wording and when it occurred.
- A screenshot or sample with personal information and secrets removed.

Do not send passwords, payment details, API keys or customer records. If the issue concerns an agreed workflow, use the support and escalation arrangements in its handover guide.
