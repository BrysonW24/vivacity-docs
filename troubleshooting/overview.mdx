## In the Vivacity app

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Connect opens nothing | Allow pop-ups for the app in your browser, then press **Connect** again. |
| An app says it is not set up | Vivacity hasn't switched that app on for your business yet. Ask us through **Book a chat** in the menu. |
| An enquiry has no draft yet | Drafts usually arrive within a minute. There is also a daily limit on AI drafts for each business. Write the reply yourself, or check again later. |
| **Approve** gives you a reply to copy, not send | Your business hasn't been checked for sending yet. Copy the reply and send it from your own email. See [emailing replies](/app/sending). |
| Something you approved didn't happen | Open the item again: it says whether it worked. If not, ask us through **Book a chat**. |

## What to include when asking for help

- The tool and type of account you use.
- The task, expected result and what actually happened.
- The error wording and when it occurred.
- A screenshot or sample with personal information and secrets removed.

Do not send passwords, payment details, API keys or customer records. If the issue concerns an agreed workflow, use the support and escalation arrangements in its handover guide.
