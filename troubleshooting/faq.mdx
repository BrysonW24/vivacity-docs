<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to know about AI already?">No. Bring a task you understand and questions about what you would like help with. Setup and coaching start from your confidence and experience.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a paid AI subscription?">That depends on the task and tool. We can help compare suitable options. Any customer-paid AI subscription is separate from Vivacity's fee, and you approve it in your own account.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Vivacity a subscription app?">Our current services are AI setup, coaching and scoped automation for Australian small businesses. Setup is one-off; project work and optional ongoing support are agreed separately. See [scope and fees](/account/account-and-billing). The [Vivacity app](/app/overview) is in an invite-only pilot, with no price listed.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will the app send anything without me?">Replies wait for your **Approve**, and every action the assistant can take starts on **Ask**. The automated assistant answers a customer who texted first, using only the times, prices and facts you have given it. See [your assistant](/app/assistant).</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I email a reply from the app yet?">Vivacity checks each new business before replies can be emailed from the app. Until then, copy the reply and send it yourself. See [emailing replies](/app/sending).</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a tool in the library mean it is already connected?">No. These are educational references. An actual integration needs a suitability, account, permissions, cost and testing check for your task.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will I need to share all my business data?">No. Start with fictional or sanitised information and share only what an agreed task needs. [Data and permissions](/connectors/data-and-permissions) explains what to check.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can AI make mistakes?">Yes. Check facts, figures, assumptions and commitments before using a result. Ask for help if you cannot verify it.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens after setup?">You keep your account and take-away guide, with 14 days of setup questions after accepted handover. Additional coaching, projects or ongoing support need a separate agreement.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get started?">Use the [AI Fit Check](https://vivacityai.com.au/diagnostic), explore the [first-task guide](/getting-started/quickstart) or [contact us](mailto:hello@vivacityai.com.au).</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
