<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will Vivacity send emails or change things without me?">
    No. Vivacity is read-only at setup, and every outward action is **drafted and held for your approval**. Nothing happens until you say so. See [Recommendations & approvals](/features/recommendations-and-approvals).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my data safe and private?">
    Yes. Data is encrypted, each business is isolated, your data is never mixed with another business's or used to train third-party models, and you can disconnect or delete any time. See [Security & privacy](/account/security-and-privacy).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many tools should I connect?">
    As many as reflect how you run the business. More sources let Vivacity connect cause and effect. Most owners start with store or accounting, then add ads, CRM and inbox. See [Get the most from Vivacity](/best-practices/get-the-most-from-vivacity).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if my tool isn't supported?">
    Tell us at [**hello@vivacityai.com.au**](mailto:hello@vivacityai.com.au). Many tools can be imported manually meanwhile (e.g. via [Google Sheets](/connectors/productivity/googlesheets)).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh is the data?">
    It syncs on a schedule, and you can **Sync now** any time. See [Data & sync](/troubleshooting/data-and-sync).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can my team use it too?">
    Yes - invite teammates and control who can see which connections. See [Account & billing](/account/account-and-billing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which plan do I need for a given feature?">
    Feature availability depends on your plan. Compare plans on our pricing page, or manage yours in **Settings → Billing**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get help from a human?">
    Email [**hello@vivacityai.com.au**](mailto:hello@vivacityai.com.au) any time. Include what you were doing and a screenshot if you can.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
