## In the Vivacity app

The app reads a connected app when it needs to, rather than copying everything in on a schedule. A figure such as what you are owed says where it was read from. If it looks wrong, check the same record in that app first; nothing in it was changed by Vivacity.

## Compare with the source

Check the original file, record or provider report. Confirm the date range, timezone, units, filters and metric definitions. A summary can be incomplete even when it sounds confident.

## Check what the AI received

Did you provide all the relevant information? Was the document readable? Did the prompt ask it to use only the source, or leave room for guesses? Try a smaller fictional example to isolate the issue.

## For a connected workflow

Use its agreed refresh and error-handling instructions. Check the last successful run, relevant account, permissions and expected records. There is no universal Vivacity sync schedule across the tools in this library.

A successful connection does not prove the data is complete. Avoid relying on the result until the difference is understood.

## Ask for help

Describe the expected value, observed value, source and period using a sanitised example. Use the support route in the workflow handover, or [contact Vivacity](mailto:hello@vivacityai.com.au).
