## First checks

1. Confirm you are using the intended account and the provider's genuine website or app.
2. Read the exact error and check the provider's current help or service-status page.
3. Confirm you are authorised to grant the access requested.
4. If it is an agreed integration, check the handover instructions and what else depends on it before changing credentials or revoking access.

## Sign-in or consent problems

A blocked window, expired session or account restriction may interrupt sign-in. Check the browser message and provider guidance. Do not widen permissions simply to make an error disappear.

## Keys and tokens

A key may be expired, restricted or for a different account. Its owner should inspect it in the provider's own secure settings. Never paste it into an email, screenshot or general AI chat.

## If the connection is not suitable

A tool may lack the required access for your account, or the permissions may be broader than you want. A manual export, a smaller task or another approach may be more appropriate.
