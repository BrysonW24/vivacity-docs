## Start with a small example

Use fictional or sanitised information while learning. Do not share personal, confidential or customer information until you have authority and understand the selected product's handling of it.

## Keep your accounts under your control

You own the accounts used for setup and coaching. Enter your own credentials and payment details, use suitable sign-in protection and review permissions before linking a service.

Different providers, products and account settings can have different data use, storage and retention terms. Check the actual service and settings rather than assuming all AI tools handle information the same way.

## For custom work

Before a workflow or build uses business information, agree the systems, providers, permissions, responsibilities, review points and relevant retention or removal process. Security arrangements depend on the specific setup and must be checked as part of that work.
