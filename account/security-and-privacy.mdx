## Kept to your business

Each workspace is separate. Your records, drafts and logs are never shown to another business.

## Who handles your data

| Who | What for | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Supabase** | Stores your workspace's records | Sydney |
| **Lovable** | Hosts the app | May process data outside Australia |
| **An AI model, reached through Lovable** | Drafts a reply from an enquiry you paste in. The enquiry text is sent to it | Not confirmed yet. We will name it here |
| **Lovable Email** | Sends sign-in emails | Europe |
| **Nango** (only once a connection is switched on) | Holds your sign-in to a connected app | Outside Australia |

Vivacity does not use your data to train AI models, and does not sell it.

## What needs your approval

- Vivacity never sends a message for you. You send replies yourself.
- Nothing is written to a connected app until an owner or admin approves the exact change.
- Only owners and admins can connect or disconnect an app.

## Connected apps

Each connection has its own page saying exactly what it reads, what it writes, what is kept and for how long: [ServiceM8](/connectors/servicem8) and [Xero](/connectors/finance/xero). Neither is available yet.

## Australian privacy

Vivacity is an Australian business and handles personal information under the Australian Privacy Principles. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information.
