Your business data is sensitive. Here's how Vivacity keeps it safe and private.

## Your data

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Encrypted" icon="lock">
    Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, including connector credentials.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Isolated per business" icon="building-lock">
    Each workspace is kept strictly separate. Your data is never mixed with another business's.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Read-only by default" icon="eye">
    Connectors read; they don't change. Outward actions require your approval.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Never sold or used to train" icon="ban">
    Your data is used only to help you - never sold, never used to train third-party models.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Your account

- **Secure sign-in** with modern authentication.
- **Roles & permissions** so the right people see the right things - see [Account & billing](/account/account-and-billing).
- **Connection-level access** to keep sensitive sources (inbox, payments) limited to admins.
- **Revoke access** to any tool in one click.

## Australian privacy

Vivacity is an Australian business and handles personal information in line with the **Australian Privacy Principles**. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and connector data is removed when you disconnect a tool or close your workspace.

## The approval gate (worth repeating)

<Warning>
  Reading your data never triggers an action. Anything that sends a message, contacts a customer or changes a connected system is **drafted and held for your explicit approval**.
</Warning>

## Report a concern

Found something that doesn't look right, or have a security question? Email **support@vivacity.ai** and we'll respond quickly.
