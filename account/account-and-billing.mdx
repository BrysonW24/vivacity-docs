## Your workspace

Your workspace is the private home for your business in Vivacity. Workspaces are set up by Vivacity, by invitation, while we test the app with a small number of businesses. There is no public sign-up.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything an admin can, for the business they own |
| **Admin** | Connect and disconnect apps, approve changes before they are written to a connected app, see invoice details from Xero |
| **Member** | Use the workspace for the work they are given. Sees connection status only, with no buttons |

To add someone to your workspace, ask us. See [Questions](#questions).

## Fees

There is no plan to choose and no billing area in the app. Any fee is agreed with you in a conversation first and sent to you as an invoice or payment link. Nothing bills or renews automatically.

The subscriptions you already pay for, such as ServiceM8 or Xero, stay yours and are paid to those companies directly.

For Vivacity's current services and prices, see [vivacityai.com.au](https://vivacityai.com.au).

## Closing your workspace

Ask us to close it. When a workspace is closed, the receipts and consent records described on each connection's page are removed with it. Records Vivacity added to your own apps, such as a client in ServiceM8, stay in those apps because they are yours.
