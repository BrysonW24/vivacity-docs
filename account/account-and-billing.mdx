## Get started: AI setup and coaching

**A$500 total, one-off, for one person. No GST charged.**

This includes tool choice, guided account or app setup, practical coaching, a take-away user guide and 14 days of setup questions after accepted handover. Customer-paid AI subscriptions are separate.

You own your AI accounts and enter your own credentials and payment details. We help you understand the options before you choose.

## Go further

Custom work is quoted per project after understanding the task. Deliverables, permissions, testing, responsibilities and price are agreed before building. A subscription is not required.

## Optional ongoing support

Growth Partner work is tailored after diagnosis. Scope, cadence, responsibilities and fees are agreed in writing first.

## Founding offer

The first five eligible businesses can choose one free path: the one-person setup package or one scoped two-week workflow Proof of Value. Eligibility must be confirmed; applying does not reserve a place. Honest feedback and an agreed testimonial are requested, with no requirement for a positive endorsement.

## Payment and third-party tools

Payment details follow the diagnosis and agreed engagement, rather than your first enquiry or booking. Nothing bills or renews automatically because you read these guides or contact us. Check any third-party subscription, API usage or cancellation terms separately with that provider.
