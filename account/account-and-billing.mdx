## Your workspace

Your **workspace** is the private home for your business's data, connectors and team. Most businesses need just one. Manage it in **Settings**.

## Invite your team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Team" />
  <Step title="Invite by email and choose a role">
    **Owner** and **Admin** can manage connectors, billing and team; **Members** use what they're given access to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set connection access">
    Decide who can see sensitive connections like email or payments. See [Manage connections](/connectors/manage-connections).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Plans & features

What's available depends on your plan. Vivacity's commercial model runs across **Insight**, **Intelligence** and **Implementation** - from a one-off diagnostic through to ongoing co-pilot and hands-on build.

- Compare what's included on our **pricing page**.
- Manage or change your plan in **Settings → Billing**.

<Note>
  For current pricing and plan inclusions, see the pricing page on our website - it's always the source of truth.
</Note>

## Billing

- **Update payment details** and **download invoices** in **Settings → Billing**.
- **Change plan** any time; changes apply from your next cycle.
- **Cancel** from the same screen - your data stays available per our retention policy in case you return.

## Close or export

- **Export** your workspace data any time (see [Data & permissions](/connectors/data-and-permissions)).
- **Delete** a workspace from **Settings**; connected data is removed.
